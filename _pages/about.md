## Professional Bio

I am a PhD candidate in Psychological & Brain Sciences at Washington University in St. Louis, working with [Wouter Kool](https://www.wouterkool.com/). I study how people adaptively engage attentional control to support goal-directed behavior, with a focus on the value-based decisions that guide the allocation of limited cognitive resources. I use behavioral paradigms and computational modeling to investigate how people establish and adjust their attentional priorities.

Before coming to WashU, I received a B.S. in Applied Mathematics and a B.S. in Cognitive Science with a specialization in Neuroscience from the University of California, San Diego. I completed my honors thesis under the supervision of [Anastasia Kiyonaga](https://www.kiyonagalab.org/).
